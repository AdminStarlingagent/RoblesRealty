Good questions

Before you book.

What does the call cost?

Nothing. It's 15 minutes, free, with no obligation. You'll leave with a clear picture of your options and next steps — whether or not we ever work together.

I'm 6+ months out. Is it too early to talk?

That's actually the best time. Early conversations mean time to plan — improving credit, timing the market, prepping a home for sale — instead of scrambling. The call just gives you a roadmap for when you're ready.

Where in Idaho do you work?

Across the state. Maritza is based in eastern Idaho and works with buyers and sellers throughout Idaho — wherever your move takes you, the first call builds the plan.

¿Hablan español?

Sí — todo el proceso puede ser en español: la llamada, los documentos explicados con claridad, y la negociación. Usted siempre va a entender exactamente qué está pasando.
